# Sample questionnaire - quarterly price survey, fab chemicals and gases

*Estimated time: 6 minutes. Your answers are confidential: only averages of at least 5 companies
are published.*

## A. About your company
1. During **July-September 2026**, did your company buy or sell anhydrous hydrofluoric acid (AHF)
   delivered in the European Union?
   - Bought  - Sold  - Both  - Neither (end of survey)
2. Roughly how much AHF did your company buy or sell in that period?
   - Less than 125 t  - 125-1,250 t  - More than 1,250 t  - Prefer not to say

## B. Price paid or received (one block per material, order randomised)
**Product definition** - anhydrous hydrogen fluoride, purity at least 99.9%, bulk (ISO tank or
rail car). Not electronic-grade diluted HF, not small packages.

3. For deliveries during July-September 2026, what was the **average invoiced price**?
   - Price: ________  (no default value)
   - Currency: EUR / USD / other: ___
   - Unit: per kg / per tonne
   - Delivery terms: delivered to your site (DAP/DDP) / at the seller's plant or port (EXW/FOB) / other: ___
   - Don't know / no transaction in the period
4. Was this price mainly set by:
   - A contract agreed before July 2026  - Spot purchases in the period  - A mix  - Don't know
5. Compared with your deliveries in April-June 2026, the price per kg you paid or received was:
   - Higher  - About the same  - Lower  - No deliveries in April-June
   *(asked after Q3, so the level is reported before any idea of direction is suggested)*

## C. Optional
6. Anything unusual that affected this price (outage, new supplier, change of specification)?
   ________

---
*Design notes (not shown to respondents): no question contains a number that could act as an
anchor; the direction question comes after the level question; every price is tied to an explicit
specification, period, unit, currency and delivery term so that answers can be standardised.*
